PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your information

This policy describes information sessec.com may collect and use when you visit the website, create an account, place an order, make a payment, or contact support. We use it as needed to operate the panel, protect accounts, and provide customer support.

Information we use

  • Account details such as username, email address, and information you choose to add to your account.
  • Order details such as service, target link, quantity, price, status, and order identifier.
  • Payment and transaction details such as amount, method, reference, and verification status. Do not send card details or payment secrets in a support ticket.
  • Necessary security and usage records such as IP address, browser/device information, access time, sessions, and activity logs to protect the panel and investigate issues.
  • Messages and attachments you submit through Tickets so support can respond to your request.

How information is used

Information is used to create and protect accounts, calculate charges, process and track orders, verify transactions, answer support tickets, maintain the service, prevent abuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Providers and sharing

Information needed to fulfill an order may be sent to the provider handling that service. Necessary transaction details may be checked with a payment provider. We do not sell your personal information. Other disclosures are limited to what is needed for security, service operation, or a valid legal requirement.

Cookies, security, and retention

Essential cookies support account sessions, CSRF protection, and language preferences. We use reasonable measures to reduce unauthorized access, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information may be retained as needed for service operation, security, dispute handling, and legal requirements.

Your choices and requests

You can review and update some information in Account or request help with your information. For access, correction, or privacy requests, sign in and open a Ticket. Do not include passwords or API keys in support messages.
